Perfect Click — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

App: Perfect Click

Developer: JPod Studios

1. Introduction

JPod Studios ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Perfect Click metronome application ("Perfect Click" or the "App") handles information when you use it.

Perfect Click provides metronome, song management, setlist, arrangement, and custom click-sound functionality without requiring you to create an account.

2. Information We Collect

Perfect Click is designed to operate primarily using information stored locally on your device. This may include:

  • Songs and notes: Song titles, tempo settings, time signatures, subdivisions, swing settings, count-in settings, arrangements, and notes you create.
  • Setlists and arrangements: Custom setlists, their order, and saved arrangement sections.
  • Custom audio samples: Audio files you choose to import for use as click sounds.
  • App preferences: Selected click tones, button colours, saved colour presets, border settings, and other playback or display preferences.
  • Premium entitlement: The App may store information locally to remember Premium access status.

We do not require you to provide your name, email address, telephone number, or other personal information to use the App's core metronome features.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Locally stored information is used to:

  • Operate the metronome and play your selected click sounds.
  • Save and load your songs, notes, setlists, and arrangements.
  • Remember your preferences between sessions.
  • Support backup and restore functionality.
  • Maintain the App's Premium access state.

4. Local Storage and Backups

Your saved songs, notes, setlists, arrangements, custom time signatures, custom audio samples, and supported settings are stored on your device.

The App allows you to create a backup file and choose where to save it using Android's file-selection system. You can later select a backup file to restore your information.

Backup files may contain your saved song information, notes, settings, and custom audio samples. You are responsible for choosing where backup files are saved and for protecting them when stored or shared outside the App.

If you share a backup with another person or upload it to a cloud-storage service, that service's privacy policy and practices apply to the information you share.

Uninstalling the App or using Factory Reset may delete locally stored information. Keep a separate backup if you wish to preserve your data.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Perfect Click's core functionality is designed to work without sending your saved songs, notes, preferences, or custom audio samples to JPod Studios. We do not sell your personal information.

We do not use your saved musical information for advertising. If future versions introduce services that process information externally, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant practices.

6. Permissions and Device Features

Depending on the features you use, the App may rely on Android functionality needed to provide its features, including:

  • Foreground media playback: To keep the metronome running when you leave the main screen.
  • Notifications: To provide playback status and playback controls.
  • File selection: To import custom audio samples and create or restore backup files.

These features are used to support the App's functionality. The App does not need access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone for its core metronome features.

7. Third-Party Services and Purchases

The App may be distributed through Google Play. Google Play operates independently and may process information associated with app distribution, device compatibility, diagnostics, or transactions under Google's own policies.

If Google Play Billing is enabled in a released version, it may process Google Play purchase-related information under Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

This Privacy Policy does not replace the privacy policies of Google or other services you may use to store or share your backup files.

8. Data Security and Retention

Your App data is primarily stored locally on your device. You should protect access to your device and any exported backup files. We do not control how long copies of your backup files are retained by third-party storage providers or by people with whom you share them.

You can delete saved information using the App's available controls, including Factory Reset. You can also remove exported backup files through the file-management tools where they are stored.

9. Children's Privacy

Perfect Click is a general-purpose metronome application and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The App does not require an account or personal details to use its core features.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Perfect Click develops or if its data practices change. Updated versions will be published at the privacy-policy webpage associated with the App, with a revised effective date where appropriate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Perfect Click, its features, and getting the most out of your practice and live performances.

Getting Started

What is Perfect Click?

Perfect Click is an advanced metronome designed specifically for drummers. It allows you to organize songs, create custom time signatures, program song arrangements, and build gig-ready setlists in one convenient application.

Who is Perfect Click designed for?

Perfect Click is designed for drummers who want more control over their click track, whether practising individually, rehearsing with a band, or performing live.

How do I adjust the tempo?

Use the tempo adjustment buttons to increase or decrease BPM in increments of 1 or 5. You can also tap the displayed tempo to enter a specific BPM manually or use the TAP function to establish a tempo by tapping along.

What tempo range does Perfect Click support?

Perfect Click supports tempos from 40 to 300 BPM.

Can I adjust the click volume?

Yes. Use the on-screen volume slider to adjust playback volume directly from the main click screen. This makes it easy to change your monitoring level without relying on your phone's physical volume buttons.

Songs & Arrangements

Can I save individual songs?

Yes. Save songs with their own BPM, time signature, subdivision, count-in, and programmed arrangement settings. Your saved songs can be accessed from the My Songs section.

Can I edit or duplicate saved songs?

Yes. You can load existing songs, make changes, and save your updates. You can also duplicate songs to create variations without having to start from scratch.

What are preset song arrangements?

Preset arrangements allow you to program different sections of a song with their own tempos, time signatures, and subdivision settings.

Perfect Click automatically transitions between sections and stops playback when the programmed arrangement finishes, so you can concentrate on playing without manually stopping the click at the end of your song.

Can I program multiple tempo changes in one song?

Yes. Create multiple arrangement sections, each with its own tempo and time signature. Perfect Click handles the transitions automatically as the song progresses.

Can I organize songs into setlists?

Yes. Create setlists for rehearsals and live performances, then add your saved songs and organize them in the order you want to play them.

Can I move songs around within a setlist?

Yes. You can rearrange songs within a setlist to match your preferred performance order.

Time Signatures & Subdivisions

Which time signatures are available?

Perfect Click includes standard 3/4 and 4/4 time signatures, along with the ability to create and save your own custom time signatures.

How do custom time signatures work?

Build and save custom time signatures by creating your own upbeat and downbeat click patterns. You can configure signatures with up to 16 beats and customize the accent pattern to suit your music.

What are subdivisions?

Subdivisions add additional clicks between the main beats to help you maintain a more detailed rhythmic reference. Perfect Click supports 8th-note and triplet subdivisions.

Can I adjust subdivision volume?

Yes. You can adjust the volume of triplet subdivision notes to 25%, 50%, 75%, or 100%, allowing you to balance the subdivision clicks against the main beat.

Can I turn subdivisions off?

Yes. Subdivision settings can be changed to suit your preferences, including playing without additional subdivision clicks.

Sound & Playback

Can I customize the click sounds?

Yes. Perfect Click includes multiple built-in click tones and allows you to customize the sounds used for different beats. You can also import your own audio samples to create a click track that suits your preferences.

Can I use my own WAV files?

Yes. You can import supported audio samples from your device and assign them to your click sounds.

Can I use different sounds for different beats?

Yes. Customize the sounds assigned to different beats to create a more distinctive and useful click track.

What is the count-in feature?

Count-in provides a selected number of bars before the main click begins. This gives you time to prepare before starting a song or arrangement.

Can I use count-in with subdivisions?

Yes. Count-in works with your selected click and subdivision settings.

Settings & Backup

Can I customize the appearance of Perfect Click?

Perfect Click includes appearance settings that allow you to customize aspects of the app's visual presentation.

Can I back up my songs and setlists?

Yes. The Backup and Restore feature allows you to create a backup of your saved app data so you can restore it later or transfer it when needed.

Does my backup include custom sounds?

The backup system is designed to include your saved app data and supported custom sound files. Keep a copy of your backup somewhere safe.

What happens if I uninstall the app?

Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored songs, settings, and other app data. Create a backup before uninstalling or changing devices.

Can I restore a backup after reinstalling?

Yes. If you have a saved backup file, you can use the Restore function to recover the supported data included in that backup.

Live Performance

Can I use Perfect Click during live performances?

Yes. Perfect Click is designed to help drummers prepare and organize their songs for rehearsals and live shows. You can build setlists, program arrangements, and have your click ready for each song.

Does the click stop automatically when a song ends?

When using a programmed song arrangement, Perfect Click automatically stops playback when the final programmed section is complete.

Can I use headphones or in-ear monitors?

Yes. You can connect compatible headphones or in-ear monitors to your device using a supported wired or wireless audio connection.

Will the click continue playing when my screen is off?

Perfect Click uses background playback functionality to support continued click playback while you use other apps or turn off your screen. Android power management and device settings may affect background operation.
